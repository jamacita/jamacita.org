B2B Modular Analysis Terms

Terms version: JSL-B2B-MOD-TERMS-STAGING-01

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This page is a publication-safe staging draft for future business purchases of property-specific Jamacita Solutions analysis modules. It is not yet an operative contract and must not be used as the Terms URL for a live payment link.

1. Seller

Jamacita Solutions is the brand and trading presentation intended for this purchase surface.

The exact legal seller identity, legally required address information and any required tax or registration details remain activation-blocked until verified and approved.

2. Business purchasers only

Modular Analysis products are intended for business and professional use. By completing a future checkout, the purchaser will confirm that they are acting for an organisation and are authorised to arrange the purchase for that organisation.

3. Purchased module

The private checkout will identify the relevant property or asset, module title, price, currency and, where applicable, the analysis generation or document version.

Each purchased module is a property-specific analytical PDF. Other modules for the same property are separate products unless the checkout expressly states otherwise.

4. Analytical basis

A module may draw on direct observation, public information, information supplied by the relevant business, analytical inference, hypotheses, photographs or notes.

Where material, the analysis should distinguish observation, supplied information, inference, hypothesis and specialist-required questions.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, the module is not engineering or architectural sign-off, legal or tax advice, a safety or regulatory certification, implementation work, or a guarantee of a particular financial, operational or guest-experience result.

5. Payment

The price, currency and available payment method will be shown in the private checkout for the specific offer. Payment is due before document release unless another arrangement is expressly stated.

Applicable tax treatment will be shown or documented in the approved checkout, invoice or receipt flow once that treatment has been professionally confirmed.

6. Delivery

Following confirmed payment, the purchased module will be released using the delivery method stated at checkout.

During the controlled pilot, a final human quality check may occur after payment. Delivery may therefore take place within the stated delivery window rather than immediately.

If a secure download link expires before the purchaser can access the file, a replacement link may be requested after entitlement is verified against the Order ID and business purchase record.

7. Independent and time-separated purchases

Modules may be purchased independently and at different times. There is no requirement to buy a full set, bundle, Executive Synthesis, live readout or later advisory service.

Where a bundle-upgrade credit is offered, eligibility and calculation will be stated separately and may depend on the same property, analysis generation and eligible prior purchases.

8. Internal business use

The intended default use is internal business use by the purchasing organisation and relevant internal decision-makers or professional advisers.

Unless separately agreed, the purchaser should not resell the module, publish the complete module publicly, make it commercially available to unrelated third parties, remove authorship or rights notices, or present Jamacita methods as its own proprietary methodology.

Final licence and redistribution wording remains subject to legal approval before activation.

9. Intellectual property

Jamacita intends to retain its pre-existing methods, frameworks, templates and reusable know-how. Purchaser-supplied materials and third-party rights remain with their respective owners.

Purchase of a property-specific module is not intended to transfer ownership of Jamacita's underlying methodology.

10. Observation date and freshness

The module reflects the observation and analysis dates stated in the document. Hotel operations, staffing, physical conditions, technology, ownership or management may later change.

An older module should not automatically be treated as a current-condition certification. A materially changed property may require a refresh or new observation.

11. Corrections and technical delivery issues

An unreadable file, broken delivery link or demonstrable factual transcription error may be reported using the Order ID and relevant page reference.

Where appropriate, Jamacita may correct or replace the affected file. A disagreement with an analytical judgement does not by itself mean that the digital product is technically defective.

12. Confidentiality and public reference

Purchase of a module does not by itself authorise Jamacita to publish the purchaser or property as a named client, endorsement or public case study.

Separate permission is required where a named public reference or confidential client information would be used.

13. Privacy

Business contact, billing, transaction-reference and delivery data will be handled as described in the future operative Privacy Notice.

14. Checkout and delivery support

Payment, delivery, link-expiry, invoice or file-access questions will be handled through the future operative Checkout Support route. The Order ID will be the primary transaction reference.

Activation-locked legal fields

The following items must be inserted only after professional review and verification:

  • exact legal seller identity and required public address details;
  • refund and cancellation wording for the intended B2B digital-product flow;
  • liability cap, exclusions and insurance alignment;
  • governing law, forum and dispute framework;
  • final permitted-use / redistribution language where legal review changes the intended wording.

No payment link should rely on this staging page.
