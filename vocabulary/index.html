Vocabulary

A small operational vocabulary for reading systems without overloading them with jargon.

Surface
The visible interface through which a system, idea or institution becomes exposed to others.
Signal
A detail that points beyond itself: toward friction, coherence, priority or neglect.
Friction
The avoidable effort created when expectations, information, interfaces or responsibilities fail to align.
Coherence
The condition in which language, behaviour, environment and process support one another.
Interface
Any point where people, systems, instructions, spaces or decisions meet.
Operational readability
The degree to which a system makes its logic, next steps and expectations legible.
Trust layer
The visible and invisible cues that help people decide whether a system can be relied upon.
Contribution logic
The way input, insight and initiative are recognised, handled and made useful.
