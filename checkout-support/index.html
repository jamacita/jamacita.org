Checkout & Module Delivery Support

A focused support surface for future Jamacita Solutions modular-analysis purchases.

Last updated: 29 August 2026

The future support flow will use the Jamacita Order ID as its primary transaction reference.

Payment confirmed, but the module has not arrived

During the controlled pilot, confirmed payment may be followed by a final release check. Please wait until the delivery window shown at checkout has passed before requesting support.

When support is activated, quote the Order ID and use the business email associated with the purchase.

Download link expired

An expired secure link does not by itself cancel the underlying purchase. After entitlement is verified against the Order ID and business purchase record, a new delivery link may be issued.

File unreadable or corrupted

Provide the Order ID, module title and a brief description of the technical problem. A replacement file or link may be issued after verification.

Obvious factual transcription error

Provide the Order ID, page number and the specific item believed to be incorrect. Demonstrable factual or transcription errors can be reviewed and, where appropriate, corrected in a version-controlled replacement.

A difference of analytical opinion is not automatically a technical file defect.

Duplicate or erroneous charge

Quote the Order ID and business email associated with the purchase so the transaction can be reconciled against the payment record.

Never send full card numbers, security codes or passwords by email.

Invoice or receipt

Provide the Order ID, purchaser organisation and any legitimately required billing or tax information that was not already supplied through checkout.

Another authorised colleague needs access

A replacement delivery link may be issued to an authorised internal recipient after the original purchase and entitlement are verified. A new Delivery ID should be recorded internally for each reissue.

Discussing the recommendations

A purchased module is a digital analytical product and does not automatically include unlimited advisory support.

Where useful, Jamacita Solutions may separately offer a clarification call, management readout, Executive Synthesis or additional advisory scope.

Security

Do not send full payment-card details, passwords, access credentials or unnecessary guest / employee personal data through support.

Activation-locked support field

The verified checkout-support contact will be inserted only when the payment, invoicing and legal seller setup is approved.

This support route is not yet active for purchases.
