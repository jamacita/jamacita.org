Elsewhere

Observations across industries and market segments.

Selected shifts in positioning, structure and market behaviour that stand out across sectors and over time.

Mobility & Automotive

2016 · Uber

Uber introduces Family Profiles, extending individual ride accounts into a shared mobility and payment structure for families and trusted contacts.

2017 · Uber

Uber adds multiple destinations to a single ride, extending the trip model beyond point-to-point journeys.

2017 · MOIA

MOIA begins real-world ridepooling testing in Hanover, followed by the launch of its all-electric Hamburg service in 2019.

2018 · Volkswagen Group / SEAT

CUPRA becomes a stand-alone automotive brand

Volkswagen Group and SEAT establish CUPRA as a distinct marque, evolving the former SEAT performance designation into a stand-alone automotive brand with its own identity.

2019 · Daimler & BMW

Daimler & BMW introduce FREE NOW as the ride-hailing brand within their joint urban mobility platform, followed by the rebranding of mytaxi to FREE NOW in July 2019.

2019 · Volkswagen

ID.3 naming architecture

Volkswagen introduces the ID.3 naming architecture for its new electric model family.

Apps

2015 · Tinder

Launch of Tinder Plus with Rewind and Passport, alongside the introduction of the Super Like button following an initial test phase in Australia.

2019 · Bumble

Bumble introduces Private Detector, using AI to identify and blur potentially explicit images before they are opened.

Travel & Hospitality

2017 · MSC Cruises

MSC Cruises introduces MSC for Me, connecting app, interactive displays and in-cabin interfaces into a unified digital guest experience.

2022 · Condor

Condor introduces a striped visual identity across its aircraft and customer-facing brand system, inspired by familiar leisure and holiday imagery.

AI & Digital Products

2022 · OpenAI

“ChatGPT” transforms GPT into a conversational, chat-based companion with a familiar messaging-style interface.

2023 · Anthropic

“Claude” evolves from a model name into both the model family and the consumer-facing AI assistant identity.

Platforms & Delivery

2014 · Uber

Uber launches the uberFRESH food-delivery pilot in 2014 and introduces the UberEATS brand in 2015, evolving the pilot into a dedicated delivery service.
