Staging · not an operative privacy notice

Checkout Privacy

A deliberately narrow privacy surface for future business checkout, payment, document delivery and support.

The final notice will describe only the processing that actually occurs in the deployed payment and delivery flow.

Business contact data

Business contact name, email, organisation, role and related purchase references where needed.

Billing information

Billing country, address and tax information only where required for checkout, invoicing or compliance.

Transaction references

Offer, order, payment, document and delivery identifiers used to reconcile purchase and delivery.

Card data

Full payment-card details are intended to remain with the payment processor rather than Jamacita's internal records.

Delivery evidence

Link issuance, reissue and access evidence may be retained where needed for delivery support or transaction disputes.

Support

Relevant correspondence may be retained where needed to resolve delivery, invoicing, refund or correction issues.

Activation gates

The final notice requires a verified responsible legal entity, approved retention and rights language, confirmed provider / international-transfer treatment, and a technical check of the site's actual cookies and analytics.

No claim about cookies, tracking or data transfers should be made until the deployed configuration has been verified.
