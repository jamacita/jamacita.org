Checkout Privacy

Notice version: JSL-PRIVACY-STAGING-01

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This staging draft describes the intended data footprint for future Jamacita Solutions business checkout, payment, secure document delivery and transaction support. It is not yet an operative privacy notice.

1. Responsible entity

The exact legal controller / responsible entity and legally required contact details remain activation-blocked until the seller and operating structure have been verified.

2. Information intended to be processed

Depending on the transaction, the intended data set may include:

  • business contact name and business email;
  • organisation, hotel or property and role / function;
  • billing address, billing country and tax ID where required;
  • purchase-order or internal business reference where voluntarily supplied;
  • Offer ID, Order ID, Module / Document ID and Delivery ID;
  • payment and transaction references;
  • invoice or receipt information;
  • delivery, reissue and support correspondence;
  • access or download evidence where technically available and operationally justified.

3. Payment-card information

Full payment-card details are intended to be handled by the payment processor rather than stored in Jamacita's internal repository, Order Ledger or delivery records.

Purchasers should never send full card numbers, security codes or passwords by email.

4. Intended purposes

Information may be used to process a business purchase, confirm purchaser and organisation details, prevent payment fraud, meet tax and invoicing requirements, deliver the purchased module, reissue expired access, correct delivery errors, reconcile accounting records, answer support questions, handle refunds or disputes, and meet legal obligations.

5. Data minimisation

The pilot is intended to collect only the information reasonably needed to complete and evidence the transaction. Unrelated guest, employee or special-category personal data should not be requested through checkout or support.

6. Service-provider categories

The intended operating model may use service providers for payment processing, banking and settlement, secure cloud file delivery, email, website hosting, accounting and professional compliance support.

The final notice will reflect the actual provider roles and any disclosure obligations confirmed before activation.

7. Delivery and transaction evidence

Order, payment, document and delivery identifiers may be retained to show what was purchased, which version was released, when access was issued and whether a later reissue, correction, refund or dispute occurred.

Payment state and delivery state are intended to remain separate records.

8. Retention

Transaction and accounting records may need to be retained for legal, tax, contractual and dispute purposes. Delivery and support records should not be kept longer than reasonably necessary for their purpose.

Final retention periods remain subject to legal and tax advice.

9. Cookies and analytics

The operative notice will describe only the cookies, analytics and tracking that the deployed site actually uses.

No claim of “no cookies” or “no tracking” should be made until the production deployment has been technically verified.

10. Purchaser requests

The future operative notice will provide a verified contact route for privacy and data-related requests. Relevant identity / authority checks may be required before transaction or delivery records are disclosed or changed.

11. Payment and delivery providers

Payment and secure-delivery providers may process transaction data under their own legal roles and privacy terms. Final wording will reflect the providers and jurisdictions actually used at activation.

Activation-locked privacy fields

The following items require final professional and technical confirmation:

  • verified legal controller / responsible entity;
  • applicable legal bases and rights language;
  • international-transfer wording and provider roles;
  • final retention periods where required;
  • verified privacy contact;
  • actual production cookie / analytics behaviour.

This staging page must not be presented as the operative privacy notice.
